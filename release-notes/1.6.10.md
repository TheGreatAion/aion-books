### Reading
- **Bookmarks as ribbons:** a bookmarked page has a cloth ribbon hanging down its margin, and your other bookmarks peek from the top of the page, placed by how far into the book they are. Click one to go there.
- **What's next:** on a chapter's last page, the name of the next chapter and how long it takes at your pace. Click it to turn the page, or turn it off in Settings.
- **Chapter marks in long books:** books with more than 80 chapters now show every chapter on the progress bar.
- **Easier to move the window:** drag it from anywhere on the reader's top bar.

### Finishing a book
- **Whole covers:** the cover on the Finished page keeps its own shape instead of being trimmed.
