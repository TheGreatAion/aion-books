### Linux
- **AionBooks now runs on Linux.** Download the AppImage, which runs on most distributions and keeps itself up to date, or the .deb for Ubuntu, Debian, Linux Mint and the like.
- **The title bar follows your paper**, just like on Windows.
- **Read aloud uses the voices from speech-dispatcher.**
- **Settings explain dark mode and voices** in a way that fits the system you're on.

### Reading
- **A cleaner Aa panel:** each typeface shows a sample of itself, spacing, margins and layout are small drawings instead of words, and every choice sits on one rounded track, with the one in use named beside it.
